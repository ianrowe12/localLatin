    CANONES · NICENI Hira · XUIIII .  This canon text appears to be sourced from Tyrannius Rufinus' paraphrased versions of the canons, and therefore may not match other textual versions. See EOMIA I, 1.ii for comparison.    Et nequis clericus usuras accipiat aut frumenti uel uini ampliationem in nouo datum sex cuplum uel duplum recipi quod si faciat tamquam turpis lucri reum abiciendum , Ministris qui fenerant placuit eos iuxta formam diuinitus datam communione abstinere
