\caption{Token audit at each model's worst baseline layer (L). Pooling: Task~A test AUROC of mean pooling (Mean); mean pooling without special tokens (No spec.); SIF frequency weights with special tokens kept (SIF+spec.); SIF as published, which also drops special tokens (SIF); and mean pooling without the 100 most frequent training tokens (No freq.). Token ablation: the highest AUROC after dropping the $m$ token types that contribute most to the top principal components, over $m$ from 1 to 100 and two rankings (Drop), and the mean AUROC of five random sets of as many token types with matched training frequency (Rand.). Token-mix EV: the share of the variance of the first principal component's score across test passages that is explained by which token types a passage contains, with one mean per token type fit on training tokens (PC1), next to the mean of the same quantity over 20 random directions (Rand.). Share by token group: the part of that variance contributed by special tokens, by the 100 most frequent tokens and by all other tokens; the three sum to one. $\rho$: Spearman correlation between the PC1 score and the log token count of test passages. Components, token frequencies and token rankings are fit on training passages only.}
