\noindent\textbf{Reading guide.}
\textbf{Suff@25\%} ($\uparrow$): similarity retained with only the top 25\% attributed tokens.
\textbf{Comp@25\%} ($\uparrow$): similarity drop when those tokens are removed.
\textbf{Cmpct@0.8} ($\downarrow$): token fraction to recover 80\% of full-pair cosine.
$\boldsymbol{\rho_{\text{LOO}}}$ ($\uparrow$): rank correlation between attribution magnitude and true leave-one-out $\Delta\cos$.

\vspace{0.4em}

\noindent\textbf{Bottom line at 200 \emph{positive} pairs/model.}
\begin{itemize}
\itemsep0em
\item The ``$\geq 3/4$ metrics for both MaRC and IG on at least one T5 model'' threshold is now \textbf{cleared on 3 of 4 cells} (LaTa$\times$IG 3/4, LaTa$\times$MaRC 4/4, PhilTa$\times$MaRC 4/4). On the 200-random set it cleared on 0/4. The positive-pair slice tells a substantially stronger story.
\item $\rho_{\text{LOO}}$ now improves on \textbf{all 4} T5 cells (range $+0.19$ to $+0.58$). The ``ABTT universally lifts $\rho_{\text{LOO}}$'' claim from the original 20-pair snapshot \emph{is} supported on this slice; the regression seen at PhilTa$\times$MaRC on the random set ($-0.036 \to -0.120$) does not appear here ($-0.080 \to +0.295$).
\item The holdout is PhilTa$\times$IG: ABTT loses Suff@25\% ($0.807 \to 0.512$) and Cmpct@0.8 ($0.132 \to 0.501$). The PhilTa baseline already retains 81\% of the cosine with only the top quartile of tokens, and recovers 80\% of full-pair cosine using only 13\% of tokens --- a hard ceiling for ABTT to clear.
\item Caveat unchanged: cross-variant deltas conflate two effects (ABTT changes both the attribution scores \emph{and} the cosine being explained, and the cosine is now meaningfully lower under ABTT --- $0.56$--$0.57$ vs $0.93$--$0.95$), so these are descriptive shifts on different fidelity targets rather than apples-to-apples.
\end{itemize}
