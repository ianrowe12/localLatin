\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Anisotropy statistics.}
Contextual representations occupy a narrow cone, and the usual check is the mean cosine of random pairs \citep{gao2019degeneration, ethayarajh2019contextual}.
This statistic is not an appropriate measure of isotropy \citep{rudman2022isoscore}.
At the final layer, the variance share of the top principal direction predicts when rank-based similarity beats cosine \citep{parupudi2026anisotropy}, and effective rank moderately predicts retrieval quality \citep{rottach2025topology}.
Yet geometric statistics do not reliably predict downstream performance \citep{nastase2025geometry, kulkarni2026geometry}.
We relate both statistics to retrieval at every layer (Section~\ref{sec:depth}).

\paragraph{Outlier dimensions and the tokens that carry them.}
One to three rogue dimensions dominate cosine similarity in pretrained transformers \citep{timkey2021rogue}.
In BERT they are tied to a few LayerNorm weights and to token frequency \citep{kovaleva2021bertbusters, puccetti2022outlier}.
Clipping them improves mean-pooled sentence embeddings \citep{luo2021positional}, though multilingual BERT is anisotropic without them \citep{rajaee2022mbert}.
Decoder language models carry massive activations on starting and delimiter tokens \citep{sun2024massive}.
The original T5's encoder carries outliers that grow with depth \citep{zhao2025outlier}, and outliers persist through fine-tuning \citep{rudman2023outlier}.
In BERT most outliers sit on \texttt{[SEP]}, the period and the comma \citep{bondarenko2023quantizable}, and removing punctuation and special tokens before pooling improves sentence similarity \citep{fusterbaggetto2022anisotropy}.
We test these accounts on pooled T5 encoder states, comparing coordinate zeroing, direction removal and token dropping (Section~\ref{sec:localizing}).

\paragraph{Layerwise analysis and post-hoc correction.}
Prior T5 embedding results use the final layer \citep{ni2022sentencet5}, the first and last layers averaged \citep{huang2021whiteningbert}, or encoder and decoder states concatenated \citep{godey2024anisotropy}.
Post-hoc corrections remove the mean and top principal components \citep{mu2018allbutthetop}, whiten \citep{su2021whitening}, or down-weight frequent tokens \citep{arora2017sif}.
\citet{timkey2021rogue} apply them at every layer of four models and find that rogue dimensions obscure representational quality; our reading of the collapse as masked follows theirs.
Whitening beats zeroing outlier dimensions in a multilingual encoder \citep{haemmerl2023anisotropy}, centering alone barely changes embedding models \citep{ren2026meanbias}, and after fine-tuning isotropy corrections become ineffective \citep{rajaee2021finetuning}.
For routing, the nearest precedent is out-of-distribution detection in supervised classifiers, where low-variance components carry much of the signal \citep{kamoi2020mahalanobis, podolskiy2021mahalanobis}, and nearest-neighbor scores depend on feature geometry \citep{sun2022knnood}.
Lexical methods match or beat dense retrievers on Latin intertextuality \citep{schelb2026locisimiles}, while dense encoders win on paraphrastic reuse \citep{delaselle2026transcription}.
We use an existing projection, ABTT, as a probe.
