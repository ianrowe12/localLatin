\caption{Correlation between per-layer geometry and retrieval, one observation per model-layer, baseline mean pooling. $r$: Pearson; $\rho$: Spearman. Columns pool all 100 model-layers, the 36 layers of the three T5 encoders, or the 64 layers of the three embedding-trained models; LOMO gives the minimum and maximum Spearman over the six leave-one-model-out subsets. Mean pairwise cosine is on test passages; top-PC share and effective rank are on centered train embeddings. These three statistics are label-free. The two gaps are label-based separation indices. Raw cosine gap is the mean test cosine of same-directory pairs minus that of different-directory pairs; standardized gap divides it by the standard deviation of all test pairwise cosines at that layer. Targets are the baseline test AUROC and the ABTT gain (AUROC with ABTT at its train-selected $D$ minus baseline AUROC). The standardized gap is a signal-to-noise ratio of the kind AUROC summarizes, so it tracks AUROC by construction. ABTT AUROC varies little (0.962--0.987), so the gain correlations mirror the baseline ones. The pooled Pearson and Spearman values are both inflated by the two clusters of collapsed and healthy layers, and layers within a model are not independent.}
