\caption{Token audit at each model's worst baseline layer (L). Pooling: Task~A test AUROC of mean pooling (Mean); mean pooling without special tokens (No spec.); SIF frequency weights with special tokens kept (SIF+spec.); SIF pooling, which also drops special tokens, recomputed in this experiment's forward pass with training-only token frequencies (SIF; its AUROC differs from the SIF cells of Section~\ref{sec:geometry} by up to 0.015 over all models and layers); and mean pooling without the 100 most frequent training tokens (No freq.). Token ablation: test AUROC of mean pooling after dropping the $m$ token types that contribute most to the top principal components (Drop), where $m$ between 1 and 100 and one of two rankings are chosen by training AUROC, and the mean test AUROC of five random sets of other token types that hold at least as many training tokens as the dropped types (Rand.). Token-mix EV: the share of the variance of the first principal component's score across test passages that is explained by which token types a passage contains, with one mean per token type fit on training tokens (PC1), next to the mean of the same quantity over 20 random directions orthogonal to the top ten principal components (Rand.). Share by token group: the covariance share of that variance contributed by special tokens, by the 100 most frequent tokens and by all other tokens; the three sum to one, but a single share can fall below zero or exceed one. $|\rho|$: absolute Spearman correlation between the PC1 score and the log token count of test passages (the sign of a principal component is a convention). Components, token frequencies and token rankings are fit on training passages only.}
