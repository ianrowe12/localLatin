5. Key Findings

ABTT is the real deal. Consistently the best post-processing method across all models. SIF adds marginal value. Whitening consistently hurts.
Architecture effect, not language. mt5-base (no Latin training, 101 languages) achieves 0.987 AUCROC with ABTT (+0.157 over baseline). The anisotropy dip and ABTT's correction are structural properties of T5-family architectures.
Filtering vs ABTT (Phase 12d): Token filtering is at best a 47% solution. ABTT closes the remaining 53–76% of the gap. LaBSE (zero empty tokens) proves filtering can't explain ABTT's gains: filtering +0.02 vs ABTT +0.13 AUCROC.
Token-level retrieval attribution (Phase 12c): 89.5% of query-layer pairs show improved selectivity after ABTT. ABTT unmasks content tokens by removing PC1 noise from all token embeddings.
