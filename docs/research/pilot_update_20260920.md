# Reviewer pilot update, 20 September 2026

For Abigail, and for Tatiana and Siddique. Two changes to the review panel, both
from your reports of 16 September. Nothing stored changed: every note, every
recorded answer and every directory is exactly as it was, under the same names.

## In two lines

After you record an answer with the blue "None of the top 10" button, the button
at the bottom of the panel now reads "Next" and takes you to the next fragment
without saving anything further. The block headed "Directories created by
reviewers" is no longer shown.

## What was wrong

Tatiana reported it first. Pressing "None of the top 10" opens the box for the
CCL key, and the button in that box records your answer. That is the one and
only write, on purpose: if the button at the bottom of the panel could also save
the same decision, one answer would go into the log twice. So while the blue box
is open, the bottom button is switched off.

The mistake was that it stayed switched off afterwards. Your answer was
recorded, the receipt was on screen, and there was no way forward: no fragment
was selected as a match, so "Submit & Next" stayed grey. The only ways on were
to pick the next fragment by hand from the list on the left, or to press Skip,
which asks for a note "so the PI can follow up". That is the wrong thing to say
about a fragment you have just answered, and none of it was intended.

## What changed

**A "Next" button after you record.** Once you have recorded a "none of the top
10" answer for the fragment on screen, the bottom button reads "Next" and is
live. Pressing it moves you to the next fragment and writes nothing at all. It
is not a second save, which is why it no longer says "Submit".

The same "Next" is there when you come back to a fragment you answered this way
earlier: your recorded answer is shown back with "Change this answer", as
before, and the button below takes you on.

**Nothing moves on by itself.** Recording still leaves you on the fragment, so
you can read the sentence saying what your key did before you go anywhere. That
sentence is the only place the app tells you which of the outcomes happened.

**If you then pick a candidate, it is a save again.** Press one of the numbered
buttons after recording and the bottom button goes back to "Submit & Next" and
writes that answer as an ordinary match. Both answers stay in the log; the
second does not erase the first.

**No more note request over a finished answer.** The line asking for a note
"so the PI can follow up" belongs to Skip, which is a deferral. It is no longer
shown once you have recorded an answer and "Next" is live. Skip still needs a
note, so if you press it there, the line comes back.

**A line of explanation while the blue box is open.** The bottom button is still
switched off there, and now says why: use "Record this answer" above, or pick a
candidate. A grey button with no explanation reads as a broken app.

**The reviewer-made directories block is gone from the page.** You wrote that it
was hopelessly mysterious to most evaluators, and there was in fact nothing to
do in it: a fragment is filed into one of those directories by typing its CCL
key into the box beside "None of the top 10", never by pressing a card in that
block. It is hidden, not deleted. The directories themselves, their members and
everything the server knows about them are untouched, and the block can be put
back with a one line change if you ever want it.

**Simpler wording under the key box.** It used to explain what happens to a key
depending on whether the app already holds it, whether a colleague has grouped
fragments under it, and so on. That described machinery you can no longer see
anywhere on the page, so it is now two sentences: the box is optional, and if
you know the source, type its CCL key and it is saved with your answer. The
sentence you get after pressing Record is unchanged, and it still names exactly
what happened.

## What did not change

- The key box itself, and everything the app does with a key.
- The receipt after Record, including the wording for each outcome.
- Every stored review, note and directory. Nothing in this change edits, renames
  or removes a stored row, and the exports and the printed packets are the same.
- The fragments, the models and the rankings. This release ships no new data.

## What to do if you see it again

If a fragment ever leaves you with no live button and no explanation, that is a
bug and not something you did. Note the fragment number and tell us, and use the
list on the left to carry on in the meantime.
