    CANONES NICENI Hira XX  This canon text appears to be sourced from Tyrannius Rufinus' paraphrased versions of the canons, and therefore may not match other textual versions. See EOMIA I, 1.ii for comparison. This is the last portion of the canon attested in other traditions.    Et ne diaconi presbyteris praeferantur neque sedeant in consessu presbiterorum aut illis praesentibus eucaristiam diuidant sed solummodo ministrent Si uero presbyter nullus sit in praesente tunc demum etiam ipsis licere Aliter uero agentes abici debet
