\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Anisotropy, layer geometry, and repair.}
Pre-trained spaces occupy narrow cones \citep{gao2019degeneration, ethayarajh2019contextual} where a few coordinates dominate cosine \citep{kovaleva2021bertbusters, timkey2021rogue}, and corrections remove or rescale those directions \citep{mu2018allbutthetop, su2021whitening} or downweight frequent tokens \citep{arora2017sif}. Information spreads across depth \citep{tenney2019pipeline, liu2019linguistic}, intermediate layers can beat the last \citep{skean2025layer}, and multilingual work is rarer \citep{rajaee2022mbert}. Layerwise profiles put the mid-depth anisotropy peak in decoder-only models \citep{razzhigaev2024shape}; T5 enters that work with encoder and decoder states pooled \citep{godey2024anisotropy}, and its weak mean-pooled similarity is a final-layer result \citep{ni2022sentencet5}. Anisotropy is neither universal \citep{machina2024anisotropy} nor always harmful \citep{aitsaada2023anisotropy}, so we report geometry and retrieval separately and reuse ABTT with a train-only layer rule.

\paragraph{Latin and classical-language NLP.}
Latin has dedicated encoders and encoder-decoders \citep{bamman2020latinbert, riemenschneider2023lata} is covered by the multilingual encoder-decoder mT5 \citep{xue2021mt5}, and appears in multilingual and decoder-only embedders \citep{feng2022labse, qwen3embedding2025, kalm2025embedding}. Shared tasks target tagging \citep{sprugnoli2020evalatin}, retrieval work allusion \citep{riemenschneider2023sphilberta}, and charter work semantic change \citep{liu2024medieval}; no general benchmark \citep{thakur2021beir, muennighoff2023mteb} covers medieval Latin retrieval.

\paragraph{Text reuse in digital humanities.}
Text-reuse systems match n-grams \citep{coffee2012tesserae}, align reprinted newspapers \citep{smith2013infectious}, and add distributional semantics for allusive reuse \citep{manjavacas2019allusive, burns2021intertextuality}, ranking candidates in a closed collection. Our routing task is open-set: a witness may match no labelled fragment in the collection.

\paragraph{Faithfulness of token attributions.}
Integrated gradients \citep{sundararajan2017axiomatic} and learned masks \citep{brinnerzarriess2023marc} score tokens, and their faithfulness is debated \citep{jain2019attention, jacovi2020faithfully}. Erasure protocols \citep{deyoung2020eraser, atanasova2020diagnostic} target classification and retrieval explanation targets ranking \citep{anand2022xir}; we apply both to a bi-encoder cosine score.
